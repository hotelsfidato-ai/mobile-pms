Online payments
Parked

Taking money from guests online, by card and UPI, rather than only recording money that has already arrived.

What it will do

A deposit to hold a room

A guest pays part of the stay when booking, and it lands on the booking's bill.

A link to pay

A payment link sent to a guest or a company, settled straight onto the bill it belongs to.

Refunds and no-shows

A refund returned the way it came, and a card held against a guest who never arrives.

What it needs before it can be built
1
The owner's go-ahead. Online payments were parked by the owner on 8 September.
2
The payment gateway (Razorpay, PayU and CCAvenue are the usual choices in India) and its merchant account.
3
The account keys, kept in the server's settings, never on a screen or in a message.
Where it stands today

Payments are recorded: cash, card and UPI taken at the desk or the till are written against a bill. Nothing here takes money online.

It will work with: Folios, Cashiering, Guest messages.
